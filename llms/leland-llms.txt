# Leland

> Leland is a marketplace for ambitious people to access the coaching, classes,
> and community they need to reach career and educational goals. It connects
> individuals with vetted 1-on-1 coaches across MBA and college admissions,
> management consulting, investment banking, private equity, product management,
> law, medicine, and executive/career development, and sells structured live
> programs, bootcamps, test prep, and an AI Builder program. For organizations it
> offers AI-native talent training, agentic workflow deployment, recruiting, and
> outplacement. Leland+ is its subscription content library of video guides,
> essay and resume examples, templates, and expert-built AI tools.
